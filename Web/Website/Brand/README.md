# RAMMIT brand pack

Everything you need to show RAMMIT in a video, article, thumbnail or post.
The full guide with examples is at https://rammit-pi.vercel.app/Brand/

## What's in here

| Folder | Files | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Logo/Icon` | `RAMMIT-Icon.svg`, PNGs 16–1024 px, `RAMMIT.ico` | The app icon. Avatars, favicons, anywhere square. |
| `Logo/Mark` | White, blue and black mark on transparent, SVG + 1024 px PNG | Over your own background or a screenshot. |
| `Logo/Horizontal` | Icon + wordmark, for dark and light backgrounds, SVG + PNG | Headers, video intros, article banners. |
| `Logo/Wordmark` | `RAMMIT` in white and black, SVG | When the icon is already on screen. |
| `Colours` | CSS variables, JSON, GIMP/Krita palette | Matching the app's look. |
| `Social` | 800 px avatar, 1280×640 link card, 1500×500 banner | Ready to upload. |

## The name

- Write it **RAMMIT**, in capitals, every time. Not Rammit, RamMit or rammit.
- Tagline: **One click. Every account.**
- One line: *RAMMIT launches all your Roblox accounts with one click.*
- Short: *RAMMIT is a free, open-source Windows app that saves your Roblox accounts and opens a separate Roblox window for each one you tick.*

## Logo rules

- Leave clear space around the logo of at least one quarter of the icon's width.
- Smallest sizes: icon 16 px, horizontal logo 24 px tall.
- Use the files as they are. Don't recolour the icon, rotate it, stretch it, outline it, or add shadows or glows.
- Don't put the white mark on a light background or the black mark on a dark one.
- Don't add Roblox's logo next to RAMMIT's or make RAMMIT look like a Roblox product.

## Type

Segoe UI, the Windows font the app uses. Bold (700) for headings and buttons, Regular (400) for text.
Buttons and panel labels are in capitals. Headlines are in normal sentence case.

## Saying what it does

Say it plainly: RAMMIT opens official Roblox clients, one per account. Accounts are saved only on your PC.

Please don't describe RAMMIT as:

- undetectable, ban-proof or "safe from bans";
- a bot, farm, exploit, script or cheat tool;
- made by, approved by or affiliated with Roblox.

When there's room, add: *RAMMIT is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation.*
