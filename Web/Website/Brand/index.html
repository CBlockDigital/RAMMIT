Tagline

One click. Every account.

One line

RAMMIT launches all your Roblox accounts with one click.

Short

RAMMIT is a free, open-source Windows app that saves your Roblox accounts and opens a separate Roblox window for each one you tick.

Long

RAMMIT is a free, open-source Windows app for playing Roblox on more than one account. Save each account once, tick the ones you want and press Launch: RAMMIT opens a separate Roblox window for each, one after another. Send every account to the same game, server or private server, let RAMMIT pick the smallest or lowest-ping server, and launch through Roblox, Bloxstrap or Fishstrap. Accounts are saved only on your PC.
