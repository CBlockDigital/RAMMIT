Legal

Software Disclaimer

Last updated 11 November 2026 · Current

RAMMIT is independent of Roblox. What that means for your accounts. This page is not legal advice.

Independent project

RAMMIT is not made, approved, sponsored or endorsed by Roblox Corporation. Roblox is a trademark of Roblox Corporation.

Account risk

RAMMIT opens the official Roblox client and does not farm, auto-play, inject code or run scripts. Even so, Roblox decides what its rules allow, and those rules and their enforcement can change. The maintainers cannot promise that using RAMMIT, or running several accounts at once, will never lead to a warning, restriction or ban. You use it at your own risk.

Your PC and your logins

Saved login cookies are protected with Windows DPAPI, which stops other Windows users from reading them but not malware running as you. Keep your PC secure, and never share your Config folder or cookies with anyone.

No warranty

RAMMIT is provided "as is", with no warranty and, to the extent the law allows, no liability for damages, as set out in sections 15 and 16 of the GPL-3.0. Nothing here limits consumer rights that can't be excluded by law.

Unsigned downloads

RAMMIT's executables are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Download only from GitHub Releases and check the checksum.
