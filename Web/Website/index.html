How it works

Switching alts by hand means logging out and back in for every account. RAMMIT keeps them all signed in and opens them together.

  1. 1

    Add each account once with + Add. They're listed as Display Name (@username).

  2. 2

    Tick the ones you want. Click a row, Shift-click for a range, or press Select all.

  3. 3

    Press Launch 3 selected. A Roblox window opens for each account, RUNNING counts them up, and you can cancel halfway.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. RAMMIT is free and open source under GPL-3.0. The code, every release and its SHA-256 checksum are on GitHub.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11. Run RAMMIT-Setup.exe, or unzip the release and run RAMMIT.exe. You don't need Python.

Where are my accounts stored?

Only on your own PC, inside the RAMMIT folder.

Does it work with Bloxstrap or Fishstrap?

Yes. Pick Roblox, Bloxstrap or Fishstrap as the launcher in Settings.

Does it farm, auto-play or run scripts?

No. RAMMIT only opens official Roblox clients, one per account. What you do in them is up to you.

Is RAMMIT made by Roblox?

No. RAMMIT isn't affiliated with Roblox Corporation.
