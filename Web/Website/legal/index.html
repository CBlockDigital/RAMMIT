Who publishes RAMMIT

RAMMIT is developed in the open on GitHub, under the account CBlockDigital. CBlock Digital is the name of the project. It is not a registered or incorporated company. It is run from India by its maintainers, who publish under the project name.

For anything that shouldn't be public, such as a privacy or legal request, write to cblockadmin@gmail.com. Questions and bugs belong on GitHub: see Support.

About these documents

They describe this project's actual setup: a static website with no cookies or analytics, and a desktop app that keeps its data on your PC. They are not legal advice, and no single set of documents can guarantee compliance with the laws of every country.

The maintainers wrote these documents themselves, and a lawyer has not reviewed them. If something here is unclear or wrong, please say so through Support.
