Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026 · Current

How the RAMMIT website, app and installer handle data. This page is not legal advice.

In short

  • This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and has no forms.
  • The RAMMIT app keeps your accounts on your own PC. The maintainers never receive them.
  • The services RAMMIT relies on (Vercel, GitHub, Roblox, Microsoft) process data under their own privacy policies.
  • The only personal data the maintainers ever receive is what you send them yourself, by email or in a public post on GitHub.

Who this policy is from

RAMMIT is published on GitHub by CBlockDigital. CBlock Digital is the project's name, not a registered company. The project is run from India by its maintainers, who publish under the project name. Where a law requires a data controller or data fiduciary, it is the CBlock Digital project, and you can reach it at cblockadmin@gmail.com.

This website

The site is static HTML hosted on Vercel. It loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers, and does not use Vercel Speed Insights or Web Analytics. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical request data, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver the pages and protect the service. See Vercel's privacy policy. The maintainers do not receive a list of individual visitors.

Downloads and GitHub

Downloads, issues and discussions are hosted by GitHub. GitHub shows repository owners aggregated traffic for the last 14 days and download counts, not who you are. Anything you post on GitHub is public. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The RAMMIT app

  • What it stores: for each account you add, the Roblox login cookie, user ID, username and display name, plus your launch configs and settings, all in the Config folder next to RAMMIT.exe.
  • How: login cookies are encrypted with Windows DPAPI for your Windows user. Usernames, display names and settings are plain text. A log file (RAMMIT.log, up to about 512 KB with its backup) records actions and includes usernames, never cookies.
  • Where it connects: only Roblox services (roblox.com and its subdomains), to sign in, check every few minutes that your saved logins still work, look up servers and launch games. Signing in happens in a private Microsoft WebView2 window; RAMMIT never handles your password.
  • No telemetry: the app sends nothing to the maintainers.

The installer

RAMMIT-Setup.exe contacts only GitHub, to find and download releases, and checks each download's SHA-256 checksum. It installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\RAMMIT. When you uninstall, your Config folder and backups are kept unless you tick Uninstall Config/ folder too.

Roblox and Microsoft

When RAMMIT signs in or launches Roblox, Roblox processes your account data under its own privacy policy, linked from roblox.com. WebView2 is part of Windows and is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

If you email us

If you write to cblockadmin@gmail.com, the maintainers receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. It is used only to answer you, is not shared or used for anything else, and is deleted once the matter is closed. The mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. Never send passwords, Roblox cookies or files from your Config folder.

Your choices and rights

Because the maintainers hold no personal data from the app, you control it: remove an account in RAMMIT, or delete the Config folder. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act or US state privacy laws may give you rights over data about you. For data held by Vercel, GitHub, Roblox or Microsoft, use their own tools. To use those rights for anything the maintainers hold (which can only be an email you sent), or to raise a privacy complaint, write to cblockadmin@gmail.com. For everything else, see Support.

Children

RAMMIT is not directed at children and the maintainers collect nothing through the app or this website. Roblox accounts are subject to Roblox's own age rules and parental controls.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's GitHub history. The date at the top shows the latest version.
